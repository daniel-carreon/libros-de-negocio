# Alex Hormozi — *$100M Playbook: Proof Checklist* (2025)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto.

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. Proof (data que respalda advertising claims) es el diferenciador más poderoso porque cualquiera puede copiar una promesa pero nadie puede reproducir los mismos resultados; por eso 80% de los winning ads de Gym Launch eran solo "This product works."

2. Hay 13 elementos ordenados por potencia que hacen proof más compelling (in-person > virtual, live > recorded, raw > processed, show > tell, etc.), y cada uno que agregues funciona acumulativamente; es casi imposible tenerlos todos pero el que tengas multiplica conversión.

3. Proof funciona en cada paso del funnel (desde click hasta close hasta retention) y es una de las pocas cosas en negocio donde nunca puedes tener "demasiado"; lo crítico es capturar proof en el momento (foto antes-después, testimonio bruto, video live) porque "proof is like an animal in the wild—it may only be there for a moment."

## Por capítulo

### Cap. 1 · Start Here (pp. 1–2)

- Historia: Gym Launch en crisis (marketing costs duplicados), Hormozi revisa 5 años de ads
- Análisis: ordena por performance, encuentra 13 elementos comunes en top performers
- Descubrimiento clave: 80% winning ads → No educational, No offer-focused, No Hormozi center-stage, One story only: "This. Product. Works."
- Insight: "Anyone can copy an advertising claim, but no one can produce the exact same results to prove it!"
- Belief es spectrum: Prospect va de 100% certainty a 100% doubt; proof mueve prospect hacia certainty
- Pro tip: "Match claims to proof" no "prove claims"—mejor collect data, make claims que data ya respalda

**Cita ancla** (cap. 1): «Everyone can make the same promise, but no one can have the same proof»

### Cap. 2 · The 13-Element Proof Checklist (pp. 2–7)

**1. In-Person > Virtual**: See con ojos propios > digital
- Ejemplo: limpiar pavement en puerta cliente > demo virtual
- Acción: fly customers a eventos, spotlightearlos cerca del pitch

**2. Live > Recorded**: Live testimonial stage > same video pregrabado
- Acción: line up customers live durante pitches

**3. Raw > Processed**: iPhone testimonial sin edición > highly edited video
- Acción: da ejemplos de good testimonials, build prompting into customer journey
- Proof: gym owner raw > edited en ad performance

**4. Show > Tell**: Video split timeline (antes/después mismo frame) > describe ("unboxing videos")
- Acción: record client experiences, ask customers document results
- Proof: "171 NEW SIGNUPS AT $500" (show packed gym visual)

**5. Other People > You**: Customer testimonials > business saying own stuff great
- Lógica: "You are most biased person in existence"
- Acción: step en customer journey para ask reviews

**6. Identical to Them > Opposite**: Feature people look like customers (age, gender, ethnicity match)
- Demo: female owners ads → more female owners; múltiples avatars → show each
- Acción: intentionally represent demographics en advertising

**7. Personal > Generic**: Specific pain story (chafing thighs, ashamed, marriage sexless) > generic "I was fat then skinny"
- Acción: in interviews probing questions: "Why important? Describe conditions. What specific things happened?"

**8. Big Results > Small > No**: $250k sale > $5k profit (biggest results win); investment required en getting them
- Acción: reach out longest-serving biggest-result customers, pay/invest capture story
- Proof: Cale gym 0→200 members fully outsourced sold 18mo $250k

**9. Newer > Older**: Testimonials from during/after crash > before crash
- Acción: always updating, fresh weekly
- Proof: Amazon sort "Most recent" no "oldest"

**10. More > Less**: Sheer numbers overcome quality gaps; "floor-to-ceiling" happy reviews
- Acción: proof page con hundreds/thousands testimonials + CTA every 3-5
- Proof: Hormozi page con 5,000 testimonials, people read all night

**11. Third Party Verification > Zero**: Awards, certifications, accreditations (brand endorsement)
- Acción: install 3rd party verification en all points
- Proof: Amazon reviews en non-Amazon page, BBB endorsement

**12. Numbers > No Numbers**: People argue less plain numbers; "showing not telling" con charts/graphs
- Acción: collect data at milestones (signup, onboarding, 30-60-90 days), automate/manual
- Proof: "54.1% people Skool make money", "Gym Lord +$100k profit year 1"

**13. Metaphors > Technical Jargon**: Connect unknown to known (ex: "SEO like investment account, Ads like commissions")
- Acción: list technical components, come up with metaphor comparisons
- Proof: Skool = "online parties/clubs/cafes" not "digital communities"

**Cita ancla** (cap. 2): «Proof is like an animal in the wild. It may only be there for a moment. You have to capture it.»

### Cap. 3 · Why and When to Use Proof (pp. 7–8)

- **When do it right**: Better ROI marketing, more/quality clicks, convert traffic→leads, leads→sales, close higher prices, more upsells, sell faster, higher LTV, better cash flow, market share, team utilization, hire better, company worth more, easier expansion, word-of-mouth, loyal base
- **When do it wrong**: Lumpy sales, copied offers, wasted ad spend, lost customers competitors, missed rational customers, no upsells, unsold products, unhappy employees, trouble hiring, old tech, new products fail, no loyal base, no referrals, compete on price, can't scale, margins shrink, lose market share
- **Pro Tip: Local Proof**: Print 5-star reviews, cheap frames, floor-to-ceiling lobby; physical/digital notebook con proof en waiting room

**Cita ancla** (cap. 3): «Proof works at all steps in a business. To convince people to click on an ad. To convince people to opt in on a page»

## Para Daniel

El **Diagnóstico RACE** necesita proof desde minuto 1: la página de 3 acciones debe incluir "qué proof tienes de que esto funciona" (ej. "3 gym owners en tu tipo de nicho +$100k profit"); esto es elemento de cierre en la llamada. Para el **Acompañamiento**, el módulo "Captura de Proof" es: semana 1 audita qué proof ya tiene, semana 2 diseña sistema de captura (cliente documenta resultados, Hormozi ordena por elemento 1-13), semana 3 rebuilds ads con proof. El **Canal de Levy** puede hacer videos que sean puro proof: "Customer story (Raw, In-Person, Live tone, Numbers)", ejemplo "Gym owner went $0→$100k, here's his exact path" (puro elemento 4: show no tell). Proof es lo que diferencia un consultor nuevo (no tiene proof) de uno consolidado ( 100 case studies): Hormozi lo codifica acá.
