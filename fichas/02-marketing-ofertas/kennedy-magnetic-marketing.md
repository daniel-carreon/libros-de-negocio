# Dan S. Kennedy — *Magnetic Marketing* (2018)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto. Páginas = número de página **del PDF**
> (el que muestra el visor, no el impreso).

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. La mayoría de empresarios fracasan no por mal producto sino por no tener **un sistema confiable para atraer un flujo constante de leads calificados**; el marketing es más importante que la calidad del producto.
2. **Direct Response Marketing** ("respuesta-directa") reemplaza la publicidad institucional: cada comunicación debe generar una acción medible e inmediata, con retorno cuantificable por dólar invertido.
3. El **Magnetic Marketing System** integra tres pilares inseparables (Mensaje compelente + Mercado específico + Media donde está la audiencia) en tres fases (Atracción → Conversión → Retención) que generan "una máquina predecible 24/7/365" de clientes.

## Por capítulo

### Cap. 1 · What If Everything You've Ever Been Told About Growing a Business Was Wrong? (pp. 19–29)

- El mito central: que "mejor" (producto, servicio, actitud, habilidad de venta) es suficiente → **falso**; sin un flujo de leads calificados, nada importa.
- El cambio estratégico de empresarios es de "publicidad tradicional/institucional" a "Direct Response Marketing" = **respuesta medible e inmediata**.
- Magnetic Marketing son **principios, no tácticas**: identificar exactamente WHO es tu cliente ideal y hacerlo responder de forma específica, repetible, medible.
- Separación de competencia: la clave es responder a "¿Por qué me elige a mí vs. otros?" = **USP (Unique Selling Proposition)**.
- Los empresarios no son "tontos" sino víctimas de mitos que sus mentores les enseñaron = "Professor of Harsh Reality".

**Cita ancla** (p. 22): «Without a sufficient and steady stream of people with whom you can exchange value for money, nothing else about your business matters.»

### Cap. 2 · Why Marketing Fails (pp. 33–47)

- **Mistake #1**: Marketing to the wrong people (dirigirse a TODOS en lugar de WHO específico).
- **Mistake #2**: Saying the wrong things = "Message to Market Match" fallido; no hablar la lengua/contexto mental de tu audiencia.
- **Mistake #3**: Asking too soon (pedir venta sin construir confianza primero); necesitas "Lead Generation" para bajar el threshold resistance.
- **Mistake #4–5**: Marketing sin sistema; random media spending sin estrategia = "Rollercoaster Marketing" (boom/bust).
- **Mistake #6**: Chasing clientes en lugar de atraerlos magnéticamente (perspectiva equivocada).
- **Mistake #7–9**: Imitar Coca-Cola's budget, carrera al precio más bajo, sin follow-up = pérdida total de dinero.
- Clave: **el 5% en cualquier momento está listo para comprar; 95% cae en oídos sordos** → hay que "hit the bullseye" con precisión.

**Cita ancla** (p. 35): «only 5 percent at best, are intellectually, emotionally, practicably and financially ready to act on or make a buying decision about your product, service or»

### Cap. 3 · What Happens When You Attract Instead of Chase? (pp. 49–55)

- Switching from "chasing" a "attracting" = el secreto de negocios extraordinarios.
- Derek Emery (used cars): cambió USP a "cash in 20 min guaranteed" → pasó de 200–300 autos/mes a 1000+/mes.
- Dr. Dustin Burleson (orthodontist): se dio cuenta que su negocio NO era ortodoncia sino **marketing de servicios de ortodoncia** → creció 4 sillas 1 clínica a 4 locations 5 doctores 35 empleados.
- Dave Dee (magician): Implementó Magnetic Marketing en 90 días → pasó de 3 shows/mes a 30+ shows/mes, deuda pagada, familia liberada.
- Ben Glass (personal injury attorney): Lead generation magnet + multistep campaign → predictable daily clients (competing on value, not price).

**Cita ancla** (p. 50): «Not only will we buy someone's used car, but we will guarantee cash in hand in twenty minutes or less.»

### Cap. 4 · What This Actually Looks Like (pp. 58–69)

- Diferencia: "Goodyear blimp advertising" (image, hope, unmeasurable) vs. **"Direct Marketing"** (response, measure, ROI).
- Regla de oro: **Spend $1 → get back $2–$20 fast & tracked**. NO gastar $1 sin retorno directo/rápido/medible.
- Cita de Gary Halbert: "Hamburger stand en playa, ¿qué necesitas? A **STARVING CROWD**" → tu job es encontrar quién necesita lo tuyo.
- Cliente no quiere "drill 3/4 inch" sino "hole 3/4 inch"; más profundo: **seek underlying need** (pride, peace, confidence, advocacy, transformation).
- 15 industrias & lo que venden realmente: Financial = peace; Dentistry = confidence + attractive smile; Fitness = transformation; Retail = transformation; Coaching = empowerment.

**Cita ancla** (p. 61): «Your job is to find that 'starving crowd' who can't live without what it is you have to offer.»

### Cap. 5 · The Magnetic Marketing Triangle (pp. 75–91)

- Tres pilares equiláteros **interdependientes** (cada uno = 1/3 del poder; falla uno = colapsa todo):
  1. **MARKET**: Geographic + Demographic + **Psychographic** (qué mantiene despiertos, frustración, dolor, deseo secreto).
  2. **MESSAGE**: USP + **"Widgets"** = ofertas irresistibles que empaquetan múltiples elementos (precio, bonuses, garantía, urgencia) en algo único/invendible.
  3. **MEDIA**: Go EVERYWHERE tu WHO está (Facebook, magazines niche, radio) NOT everywhere for everybody.
- Bob Stupak (Vegas World): Widget $396 × 2 noches, suite, champagne, drinks ilimitados, $600 chips = empire.
- **3 tipos de ofertas**: Lead Generation (free report), Consultation (meeting), Direct Purchase (final ask).
- Lead Generation Magnet: 4 jobs: (1) enhance response, (2) reinforce pain, (3) establish expertise, (4) position para aceptar recomendación.

**Cita ancla** (p. 83): «With this widget, he managed to create a Godfather-level kind of offer—the kind of offer you simply cannot refuse.»

### Cap. 6 · The 10 Foundational Rules (pp. 97–111)

1. **ALWAYS an offer** (irresistible, time-sensitive) OR you're wasting money (no implied offers).
2. **REASON to respond RIGHT NOW** = urgency (deadline, limited #, remove bonus, bidding).
3. **CLEAR instructions** → "confused buyer won't buy"; walk step-by-step.
4. **TRACKING, MEASUREMENT, ACCOUNTABILITY** → forget likes/views; only cash ROI.
5. **ONLY NO-COST brand building** → buy response, accept brand as bonus (NOT vice versa).
6. **FOLLOW UP** → "almost persuaded" es la mayor pérdida; Restate offer, 2nd notice, 3rd & final notice, change offer.
7. **STRONG COPY** = backwards from customer (not forward from company); emotional (not factual); bold claims (not timid).
8. **LOOKS LIKE MAIL-ORDER advertising** → headline/subheads (top 1/4), product/testimonials (middle 1/2), offer/CTA (bottom 1/4) or advertorial or sales letter (4–64 pages).
9. **RESULTS RULE. PERIOD.** → Split-test, A-B test, Test-Monitor-Adjust. Opinions DON'T count; only customer ballots = cash.
10. **TOUGH-MINDED DISCIPLINARIAN** → Direct marketing diet; bar the door to anything that breaks the 10 rules.

**Cita ancla** (p. 110): «Do not let anyone confuse, bamboozle or convince you that anything else is of any importance. Nobody's opinion counts—even yours.»

### Cap. 7 · Your Magnetic Attraction System (pp. 114–123)

- Sistema: **Media channels → Lead Generation Magnet → Leads (raise hand) → Follow-up → Conversion**.
- Lead Generation Magnet (LGM): información valiosa que atrae exactamente WHO quieres (free report, checklist, cheat sheet).
- 4 jobs del LGM: (1) enhance response, (2) reinforce pain, (3) establish expertise + empathy, (4) position favorablemente.
- Evitar "me, me, me" speak = BE YOU-oriented; No overeducate = TELL what, not how; **OBLIGATORY CTA** (call to action).
- Powerful titles = Model tabloid/magazine headlines (Enquirer, Cosmopolitan, Readers Digest).
- Landing page / Squeeze page = very simple, only collect contact info (no navigation, no distractions).
- "Raise their hand" = primer YES, pequeño pero crucial; cruzan primer threshold.

**Cita ancla** (p. 85): «This is an offer whose only purpose is to, in effect, entice a prospect to raise their hand to identify and register themselves as having»

### Cap. 8 · Your Magnetic Conversion System (pp. 125–137)

- Goal: interested → paying customer.
- Overriding principle: **"Show Up Like No One Else"** (stand out, different, special, heightened value).
- Higher income = pay for WHO you are (unique), NOT WHAT you do (commodity).
- MULTISTEP CAMPAIGN: small, manageable target market + series of communications (letters, emails, postcards) = repetition = impact.
- Model: 1st notice, 2nd notice (15 días después, linkage), 3rd & final notice (urgency) = from bankruptcy collection industry.
- ADDING CREDIBILITY: Content series (video training, reports, assessments, books) = extend sales timeline, build authority.
- "SHOCK AND AWE": Bundle high-value items (reports, DVDs, testimonials, assessments, gifts, candy, toys) → overwhelm with proof.
- CONSULTATION vs. DIRECT SALE: Series of micro-agreements (YES to LGM, YES to video, YES to book) = habit of saying YES.
- **"Until they buy or die."** = No rendirse; continue with conversion system.

**Cita ancla** (p. 126): «Show Up Like No One Else.»

### Cap. 9 · Your Magnetic Retention System (pp. 136–144)

- "This is the part of the puzzle that no one does" = turn 1 client into 2 (double value).
- **CUSTOMER NEWSLETTERS** (monthly, preferably printed):
  - Higher perceived value than email.
  - More likely to be read & retained.
  - Lingers as ongoing reminder on file cabinet.
  - Content: USP/expertise (minimal), fun (puzzles, jokes, recipes), "What's new?" widget CTA, personality/stories.
- **REFERRALS / WORD OF MOUTH**: "What others say is 10× more believable than what you say."
- Walt Disney principle: "Do what you do so well that people can't resist telling others about you."
- Create "WOW experience" = staff stands up, shakes hand, welcomes by name → memorable.
- **Earn the right to ask** (after praiseworthy act); present card/form + "give me 5–6 names" = 9 de 10 veces 3–4 nombres.
- Chiropractor example: back-care class + referral forms = 7/10 patients fill out, 3–10 names each, 1/3 become new patients.

**Cita ancla** (p. 140): «The way to be very successful in marketing is to do what you do so well that people can't resist telling others about you.»

### Cap. 10 · Applying the Magnetic Marketing Systems (pp. 149–159)

- **Al the Plumber case**: real-life proof que "if a plumber can do it, you can do it."
- **Endorsed mailing to champion circle of influence** = ONLY mail 100% delivered + 100% opened + 100% read (from friend's stationery, not vendor's).
- **Lead Generation Magnet**: "Free Home Plumbing Problem-Prevention Audit" (lowered threshold thanks to endorsement).
- **Multistep campaign** (3 letters, 10 días apart):
  - Letter 1: Intro + LGM offer.
  - Letter 2: Article reprint (elderly couple's house flooded from small leak).
  - Letter 3: "Final notice" + 72-hour deadline + list of plumbing disasters.
- **Results**: 300 letters → 72 audits done (24% conversion) = $300 × 3 letters cost.
- **Show up like no one else**: 3-piece suit, white shirt, tie, calfskin briefcase, clipboard, DVD (about elderly slipping hazard).
- **At home**: DVD plays → $389 upsell (5 baths anti-slip product).
- **Follow-up**: Stickers on pipes + monthly newsletter "Plumber's Helper" = "Who will they call?"
- **Parable of the bird in your hands** (Gerry Spence / Yogi Berra): Whether you act or not is entirely in your hands; reader who doesn't act = no better off than illiterate.

**Cita ancla** (p. 151): «It's the only piece of mail on the planet that will 100 percent get opened and 100 percent get read.»

## Para Daniel

**Acompañamiento**: El Capítulo 6 (10 Rules) + Capítulo 10 (Al the Plumber case) son puro script de **Diagnóstico RACE** → 20–30 min con dueño, entregar página con 3 acciones. La estructura "endorsed mailing → multistep → upsell" cabe perfectamente en una semana de implementación para que el consultor asesore y cobre en la llamada. El vocabulario de "WHO" (psychographics: qué los mantiene despiertos, frustraciones, secretos deseos) es exact diagnosis del cuello de botella del negocio.

**Canal de Levy**: Episodios 1–3 podrían ser "por qué falla el marketing" (9 mistakes, pp. 33–47) + "qué es direct response" (pp. 58–62) + "cómo encontrar tu WHO" (pp. 75–81). Episodio 4: case study completo (Al the Plumber, pp. 149–159) como prueba de concepto en micro. Entretenido, educativo, manda a Arbrain con ganas de aprender Magnetic Marketing.
