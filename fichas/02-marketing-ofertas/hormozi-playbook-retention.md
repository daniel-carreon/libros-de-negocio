# Alex Hormozi — *$100M Playbook: Retention* (2025)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto.

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. Reducir churn de 10% a 3% = 3.3x LTV sin más clientes; es 5-25x más barato retener que adquirir, y pequeños cambios (+5% retention) = grandes ganancias (+25-95% profit), haciendo retention el lever más ROI-positivo para scaling

2. Los "5 Horsemen of Retention" (track attendance, reach out 2x/semana, handwritten cards, member events, exit interviews) redujeron churn 15%→3% en gyms; cada uno salva pocos clientes pero sumados generan "montañas de cash" porque la retención compone (happier customers = más referrals = growth sin más CAC)

3. Las 9 acciones del Churn Checklist (find activation points, onboard, incentivize activation, community linking, fire bad customers, annual pricing, cancellation calls, surveys, customer journey 4-milestone) son tareas de "high leverage" que toda empresa debe implementar una sola vez bien para generar compounding growth: ingresos recurrentes sin adquisición nueva

## Por capítulo

### Cap. 1 · The Problem and The 5 Horsemen (pp. 1–2)

- Contexto: Gym Launch 1,000 gym owners, 15% month-over-month churn = 83% anual; con 250 miembros Jan 1 need 208 new by Dec 31 solo stay same; "leaky bucket business"
- Momento: Hormozi applied DoD (Department of Defense) problem-solving process: ask experts, get answers, find common factors
- Research: interviewed gym owners con <3% churn, encontró 5 common factors

**5 Horsemen of Retention** (todos críticos, orden no importa):
1. **Track Attendance**: <3 sessions/week = churn risk; reach out inmediatamente cuando cae = rescue
2. **Reach out 2x/week**: Praise participation + solve problems = sticky
3. **Handwritten Cards**: Sign-up, 3-6-12 months, holidays, birthdays (must be handwritten, personal touch)
4. **Member Events**: Regular calendar every 21-42-63 days (random to member pero scheduled), handwritten invites, reduces churn AND gets referrals
5. **Exit Interviews**: If say leaving, interview before → save ~50% phone/email cancellations (= 33% LTV increase)

**Results**: Month 1 churn UP 50% (shaking tree), Month 2 DOWN 50%, Month 3 DOWN 50% (15%→7%→3% = 3.3x LTV)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 1): «I have yet to see a business with less than 3% churn that does not make great money»

### Cap. 2 · Understanding Churn and Its Economics (pp. 2–4)

**What is Churn?**
- Customers who *leave* over period; if 100 customers last month → 95 this month = 5% churn (or 95% retention)
- Only counts original pool (new signups during period don't affect churn %)

**Price-Value-Churn Formula**
- Warren Buffett: "Price is what you pay. Value is what you get."
- If Value > Price → stay; if Value < Price → churn (visual: "Point of Cancellation")
- Reverse engineering: instead of "How retain?" ask "What make 100% churn?" (ignore, break promises, miscommunicate, treat poorly, hide progress, hide happy customers, make harder use) → DO OPPOSITE

**Relationship Between Price and Churn**
- Higher price → higher churn generalmente, pero maybe better LTV total (test)
- Best price = most sales at highest LTV (example: $20 beats $10 y $100 porque balance conversión y churn)

**Provide Ongoing Value**
- "Value per second not seconds of value"; less but better > more decent
- Overwhelm = #1 churn reason
- Example: newsletter guy $500k/month con 1 Q&A call + 1 long newsletter (when added more things, churn UP)
- Gym Launch 10 versions cada shorter/simpler

**Why Reducing Churn So Important (3 razones)**
1. **Best returns on effort**: 5-25x más caro acquire vs retener; budget: 1/5th CAC on retention
2. **Tiny changes = huge profit**: Harvard Business Review +5% retention = +25-95% profit (1 month → 1.05 months average)
3. **Only path compounding**: Si referrals > churn, never stop growing (10% churn + 10% referrals = flat; decrease churn = growth + happier customers = more referrals)

**Before/After (10% → 3% churn)**
- Old: $100/mo, 10% churn, LTV $1,000
- New: $100/mo, 3% churn, LTV $3,333
- Effort to 3.3x LTV via acquisition = 3.3x más clientes = way más cost y market saturation
- Simple: Churn is invisible hand suppressing growth

**Cita ancla** (cap. 2): «Churn is the invisible hand that suppresses your growth»

### Cap. 3 · The 9-Step Churn Checklist (pp. 5–8)

**1. Figure Out Activation Points** (MOST IMPORTANT)
- Leading indicator: "Every customer that does X or gets Y stays longer"
- Skool example: hosts con 3+ miembros stay way longer
- Process: find churned, find who stayed 3+ months, top 20% por revenue, learn demographics/psychographics, find common 5 factors
- Result: update messaging ads onboarding to select perfect customer only
- Gym Launch proof: 70x more profit ($5k → $42k LTV) targeting solo highest-retention avatars

**2. Onboard Your Customers** (teach to hit activation)
- Custom > generic, personal > group, live > recorded, carrots > sticks, **some beats none**
- Best onboarding: resell value in context goals, tell homework + deadline, tell what unlock, tell best practices communicate, schedule next call, follow up
- Optional: sell onboarding (6 personal training before general classes = "earn right")
- Portfolio example: no onboarding → onboarding → 1-on-1 = 25% ascension boost, $2M/mo → $2M/week

**3. Incentivize Customer Activation** (make worth it)
- Options: features, recognition, free/discount, VIP access
- Skool incentives: unlock courses, 1-on-1 consulting, event tickets, higher tier calls, custom badges, Level 8 = lifetime free
- Also: put cool unlocks just after major churn points (if leave month 3, cool thing month 4)

**4. Community Linking** (connect members to each other not to you)
- "Easy quit membership, hard leave relationship"; "Come bikini stay community"
- Group events (1-on-1 → 1-on-6 onboarding intentionally connects people), manually introduce people, start podcast, elevate micro-celebrities

**5. Correct or Fire Bad Customers** (they poison pool)
- Delete bad posts + tell why, 3 strikes rule
- Pin best 1-2 daily (signal what reward, helps others level up)
- 4 topic categories: wins (testimonials), fun (light), discovered (proof+data), meetups (connections)

**6. Add Annual Pricing Options** (customers pay longer → stay longer)
- Website: 10-20% take annual if "buy 10 get 2 free" (16% off)
- Phone/webinar: annual mandatory better
- Pricing: above avg months if <12; if >12, 16% off
- **Alternative 1**: Big upfront 1-time (education/setup) + small monthly (consumable) = "big head long tail"
  - Example: $6,800 upfront + $199/mo = 30 months LTV = $12,800
  - Key: include recurring WITH upfront NOT separate sale (price anchor)
- **Alternative 2**: "Founder rates" (50% off for business owners)

**7. Exit Interviews / Cancellation Videos** (save ~50%)
- Phone > email/text
- Techniques: get upset than them, validate, "Will give opportunity make right?", resell on goals, remind what losing
- If high volume low price: cancellation VIDEO (resell + remind risk losing)

**8. Survey Customers 2x/year** (what value, what don't)
- Questions: "If removed everything but one, what keep?" + "If kept everything but one, least bothered remove?"
- Also: reach out 1-on-1 every 2-3 weeks (ACA: Acknowledge, Complement, Ask)
- Counter-intuitive: wealthier customers want less handholding

**9. Customer Journey** (4 Simple Milestones)
- Activate → Testimonial → Refer → Ascend
- Order can vary except activate first ideally
- Incentivize each milestone
- Ascension: customers itch buy more over time (sell them more!)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 3): «If your LTV increases you can spend more than anyone else to get more customers»

## Para Daniel

**Acompañamiento**: Módulo "Retención = Compounding" es: encontrar activation point del cliente (cap. 3, #1), diseñar onboarding (cap. 3, #2) que drive ahí, implementar 1-2 de los 9 items cada semana (cascada de implementación). Semana 1: activation + onboarding. Semana 2: incentivize. Semana 3: community. Etc. Al final: 9 items = compounding, "never marketing only growing."

**Diagnóstico RACE**: Primera pregunta: "¿Cuál es tu churn?" Si dicen "no sé" = #1 acción es medir. Si miden, lápiz #1-3 del checklist en 24h (activation point es lo crítico). La página de 3 acciones: "(A) Reduce churn from X to Y target, (B) do exit interviews, (C) track attendance."

**Canal de Levy**: Videos "Retention Stories"—gym owner cambió de churning fast a 3% churn because implemented 5 Horsemen (short stories, dramatic before-after en churn %, emotional). Esto vende el Acompañamiento porque proof es tangible (churn metric public = computable).
