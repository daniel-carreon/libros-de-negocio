# Daniel Kennedy — *The Ultimate Sales Letter* (1991)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto. Páginas = número de página **del PDF** (el que muestra el visor, no el impreso).

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. El sistema definitivo de Kennedy: 28 pasos exhaustivos para escribir sales letters efectivas que compiten exitosamente contra cualquier táctico, sin importar experiencia previa.
2. La escritura de ventas es un oficio enseñable basado en entender profundamente al cliente, el producto, y cómo provocar acción mediante storytelling, garantías y secuencias.
3. Las secuencias de tres cartas (no mailing único) son el "secret de un millón" del libro: repetición timed en 45-60 días que dispara respuesta directa.

## Por capítulo

### Sección I: Before You Write a Word (pp. 1–17)

- No necesitas maestría: la ventaja tuya es entender tu negocio mejor que cualquier copywriter freelance
- Build "swipe files" (idea files): colecciona muestras de ads, mailers, sales letters de otros
- Piensa venta: una sales letter es una presentación de ventas en papel; cómo venderías si estuvieras en la sala
- La mentalidad de escritura: "fear to confidence; difficult to easy; incompetent to competent"

**Cita ancla** (p. 13): «This is a tremendous advantage and you, my friend, possess it.»

### Step 1: GET "INTO" THE CUSTOMER (pp. 11–23)

- 10 Smart Market Diagnosis Questions: qué los mantiene despierto, qué los enoja, qué desean secretamente
- Usa Maxwell Maltz "Theater in Your Mind": visualiza los recipients como seres vivos respirando, pensando, sintiendo
- Error universal: hablas tus prioridades, no las del cliente
- Ejemplo Arthritis Foundation: carta dirigida a beneficios corporativos (TV exposure) no necesidades de fundación = 0.5% response pero donors importantes

**Cita ancla** (p. 21): «If you can really see your prospect, you can communicate successfully with him in print.»

### Step 2: GET "INTO" THE OFFER (pp. 19–29)

- Build feature/benefit list en 3x5 cards; shuffle por importancia
- **Key insight: "People do not buy things for what they are; they buy things for what they do."**
- Hidden benefit (Ted Nicholas concept): Recruiting system vendido como "Puts Recruiting On Auto-Pilot So You Can Go Play Golf" → anuncio más rentable de cliente
- Deberías estar obsesionado con tu producto como lo estaría un salesman

**Cita ancla** (p. 27): «People do not buy things for what they are; they buy things for what they do.»

### Step 3: CREATE A DAMAGING ADMISSION (pp. 23–25)

- Usa 3x5 cards para cada objeción/pregunta/miedo potencial del prospect
- Ejemplo: phone-sex business triplicó respuesta añadiendo: "Discretely billed to your credit card as 'XYZ Supply Company'"
- Italian restaurant invierte desventajas en ventajas: "no white linen, red-white checked tablecloths, pero auténtica pasta casera"
- Aumenta credibilidad al admitir los flaws

**Cita ancla** (p. 32): «By admitting and openly discussing the drawbacks to your offer, your credibility stock goes way up.»

### Step 4: GET YOUR SALES LETTER DELIVERED (pp. 27–39)

- **10-30% of bulk mail nunca llega**: postal carriers deliberadamente descartan mounds (hay "junk drops" en edificios abandonados)
- Postal audit 1988: 84% of sites deliberadamente destruyen correo
- Estrategias: first-class > bulk; live stamps > meter; individual addressing > labels; "sneak-up" approach (plain white, sin business name, stamp, directed address)
- General Cassette Corporation: red-white-blue express letter = 82% response (postal workers la trataron como express mail)

**Cita ancla** (p. 41): «America sorts its mail standing over a wastebasket.»

### Step 5: GET YOUR SALES LETTER LOOKED AT (pp. 35–37)

- Gary Halbert wisdom: people throw mail unopened, sort con dedo sobre wastebasket
- Plain white envelope + doctor's name as return = doctor opens himself (no staff screening) = 300% higher response
- Fulfill the envelope's promise: si dice "personal", debe ser personal dentro
- Include "lift letter" from trusted source: "info has been of immense value to me"

**Cita ancla** (p. 43): «If your envelope says "Personal" outside, there had better be something "personal" inside.»

### Step 6: GET YOUR SALES LETTER READ (pp. 39–55)

- AIDA formula: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
- **Headline structures**: "They Didn't Think I Could But I Did", "Who Else Wants?", "How to...", "Are You?", "Secrets of...", "Warning:", "Give Me X and I'll Y"
- Executives: prestige words, quality paper, sealed enclosures en segundo sobre
- Mass market: short attention span, involvement devices (scratch-offs), color
- Direct sales: testimonials > drawings; proof ease-of-use

**Cita ancla** (p. 45): «By immediately saying something that is recognized by the recipient as important and valuable and beneficial.»

### Step 7: BEAT THE PRICE BUGABOO (pp. 56–66)

- **Three price-minimizing tactics**: apples-to-oranges comparison; sell bulk (1,001 benefits list); discuss R&D cost paid to develop
- Make parts worth more than whole (fair-like pitch): list seminars=$1200, marketing kit=$3000, tapes=$1000 → total $5200 but entire package $3895
- **Three formulas to transcend price**:
  1. Problem-Agitation-Solution (most powerful; fear of pain > desire for gain)
  2. Fortune Telling (predict future urgency; Doug Casey crisis investing)
  3. Winners and Losers (5% successful vs. 95% broke; bandwagon effect)

**Cita ancla** (p. 62): «The formulas are easy to understand, adaptable to many business situations, and—most important—they work.»

### Step 8: REVIEW WINNING COPYWRITING TECHNIQUES (pp. 68–78)

- **Intimidation tactics**: limited number available; most will buy (bandwagon); only special individuals; you can only buy if...; only some qualify; strong credibility copy
- **Demonstrate ROI**: "selling money at a discount"; doctors earn $10K–$25K → $199 cost = 500% ROI
- **Ego Appeals**: status symbol positioning (fax = "behind the times?"); used universally in tech/luxury
- **Strong Guarantees**: money-back; refund+keep-premium; redundancy; free trial; guarantee the letter itself ($25 payoff = high response); Cavett Robert: "make customer see hearse at door"

### Steps 9–17: The Writing Process (pp. 79–125)

- Write first draft sin editing; don't worry length/grammar (likely 2-3x longer than final)
- **Long copy > short copy**: impulsive readers need headlines/subheads; analytical readers want 50-500 word research
- Double readership path: impulsive skim headlines/boxes; analytical read full; both get offer
- **Internal repetition** (7x rule): straightforward + example + story + testimonials + quote + expert + numbered summary
- Frustrate English teachers: conversational, nonsentences, one-word exclamations = action/momentum
- Tease at end of each page: never end with completed sentence; teaser copy = headline for next page
- Style: short paragraphs (3-4 sentences); personality matters; "cold fish" letters rarely work
- Passion essential: emotional logic; enthusiasm; find something exciting about product (worst case: money/time it saves)

**Cita ancla** (p. 122): «There is a way to give your sales story a passion injection.»

### Steps 18–27: Refinement & Production (pp. 127–150)

- Compare draft to examples in book; pretest: read aloud to typical customers; use child to check readability
- Cool-off period (3-5 days) before final review: restore objectivity
- Mock-up: mail to yourself in normal context; see how it appears in real mail environment
- Aggressive editing: cut every word not advancing sales story; clarify automatically shortens
- Graphic enhancement: bullets, numbering, underlining, boldfacing, varied type, handwriting, boxes, yellow highlight, subheads
- Printer errors (PEs) vs. author alterations (AAs): fix PEs free; AAs expensive; don't move commas now

**Cita ancla** (p. 132): «You don't want anything less than a completely compelling sales letter.»

### Step 28: MAIL! (pp. 151–155)

- Narrow mailings (<1000): real stamps not meter; type/hand-address envelopes; rubber-stamp imprints; consider courier/UPS; innovative packaging (bears with cassettes = 140/200 response)
- Big mailings: avoid "OCCUPANT/SALES MANAGER" (screams junk); use imaginative addressing; strong teaser copy; celebrity ID (Ed McMahon)
- Different targets need different weapons; analyze carefully

**Cita ancla** (p. 146): «Different targets require different weapons. Take the time to analyze your target carefully—to understand your customer.»

### Sección III: The Most Versatile Sales Tool of All (pp. 161–173)

- **8 uses**: create qualified leads; support telemarketing; create store traffic; stimulate referrals; introduce new products to existing customers; sell direct; post-purchase reassurance (buyer's remorse); all persuasive communication
- **Key insight**: underutilize own customer lists; easier to sell more to known customers than get new ones
- Example hospital supply company: broke customer list by what products they bought/didn't; segmented letters = 2-3% order rate + $1 gross profit per letter

**Cita ancla** (p. 149): «When someone picks up the phone and calls you in response to your sales letter, you know you've got a pretty qualified prospect!»

### Sección IV: The Million-Dollar Sales Letter Secret—The Power of a Sequence (pp. 177–182)

- **Secret**: create sequences not one-shots
- 45-60 day timed sequence > Madison Avenue slow repetition
- Borrowed from collection industry (dunning letters: first notice, second notice, third notice)
- Giorgio's Italian Grotto example: three-letter sequence over April-May to husbands
  - Letter 1: romantic dinner + rose + candlelight ($59.95)
  - Letter 2: 3 coins + limo added (agitate problem + irresistible offer)
  - Letter 3: final push
- Response from letters 2+3 often **doubles** response from letter 1

**Cita ancla** (p. 164): «The creation of sales letter sequences rather than just creating sales letters is as responsible as any other, single idea for my becoming a millionaire.»

### Sección V: "High-Tech" Sales Letters (pp. 185–187)

- Broadcast fax: 1-2 pages, lead generation; all tactics apply
- Fax-on-demand: no length limits, no color/photos
- Internet sites: long copy works (17-25 min avg visit); download
- Auto-responders: programmed email sequence (24h, 72h, 5/10/15 days)
- E-mail: if unasked = brief (headline in RE line); requested = full length
- **Warning**: tech people ≠ marketing people; don't confuse technical advice with marketing validity

**Cita ancla** (p. 172): «A strong sales letter is a strong sales letter is a strong sales letter regardless of the delivery medium being used.»

## Para Daniel

Kennedy ofrece el sistema completo (28 pasos) que diagnostica exactamente dónde falla un lead magnet o carta de prospección. Para **Acompañamiento**: el framework de Step 1 (10 Smart Questions) es pregunta-diagnóstico pura; los 8 usos (Section III) cubren desde landing page → email sequences → referral generation → retención. Para **Diagnóstico RACE**: los 3 copywriting formulas (Problem-Agitation-Solution, Fortune Telling, Winners/Losers) son estructura de pitches de alto cierre; la garantía como "primary focus" (Step 8) es cierre. Para **Canal Levy**: un video sobre "cómo el electronic parts distributor en Arizona usó passion letter rojo-y-rosa para revolucionar su industria" directamente enseña que industria "aburrida" sigue las mismas leyes de persuasión que sexy.
