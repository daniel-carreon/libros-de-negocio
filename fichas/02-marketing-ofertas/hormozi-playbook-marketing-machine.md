# Alex Hormozi — *$100M Playbook: Marketing Machine* (2025)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto. Citas = fragmentos originales en inglés.

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. Negocios dependientes del founder (keyman risk) son menos valiosos y generan peores ads; la solución es sistematizar generación de proof customer-based.
2. Marketing Machine = checklist de 9 nodos (Lifecycle, Social Media, Events, Communication, Bonuses, Awards, Competitions, Delivery) que convierten customer journeys y resultados en ad assets automáticamente.
3. Empresas con sistema instalado no necesitan founder en cámara; obtienen más ads, mejores ads, frescos cada semana, sin hacer "más trabajo" (aprovechar customer activity ya existente).

## Por capítulo

### Prólogo · Anécdota: de keyman risk a $46.2M deal

- Gym Launch valuación baja por "keyman" (depende de cara de Alex)
- Insight: "If I got hit by a bus today, what keeps business growing tomorrow?"
- Solución: "Bottom of list feeds top of list" → usar customer testimonials en ads
- Ejecución: record calls, award events, referrals = customer-generated ad gallery
- Resultado: ads outperform su direct-to-camera, llegó a 80% customer-generated content en top 50 ads
- 12 meses después: bankers convencidos, cierra $46.2M

**Cita ancla** (Prólogo): «If I got hit by a bus today…_ _What would keep the business growing tomorrow?_ --This question has a two-part answer.»

### Cap. 1 · Why This Matters + How Machine Works: Valor y sistema

- Empresa sin founder face = más valiosa que con founder face
- Founder dancing on camera ≠ negocio real; sienten como "arrow spinner"
- Data: 40 de 50 top ads Gym Launch sin su cara (80%)
- Machine = checklist donde cada item = reliable stream de videos/imágenes/copy
- 9 componentes (nodos): Lifecycle, Social Media, Events, Communication/Communities, Bonuses/Awards, Competitions, Delivery
- Uso: paid ads, content, outreach, sales pitches, webinars, video sales letters
- Start 1-2 nodos; agregar gradualmente

**Cita ancla** (Cap. 1): «They are the star of the story. So - put them in the spotlight, not you.»

### Cap. 2 · Lifecycle Ads + Social Media Scrape: Capture moments + hashtag system

- Lifecycle: harness moments de recorded calls (before/during/after customer journey)
- Before: sales calls (hard times) | During: support calls | After: upsell/award calls (victory)
- Advantage: SHOW + TELL entire journey; raw reactions (kitchen reveal, scale victory)
- Local: "Upon Delivery Ads" = ribbon cutting moments
- Social Media: hashtag strategy (#gymlord, #poweredbyprestige) como filter
- Grab tagged media system (especialmente 24h stories); tag/save searchable; separate studs from duds
- Good organic performance = good ad correlation

**Cita ancla** (Cap. 2): «These are among the most organic videos you can capture»

### Cap. 3 · In-Person Events: 3 tipos de testimonial

- Stage ads: photo/video on stage with celebrators (proof of size/scope)
- Professional testimonials: side room + camera team, 6-point script (Internal struggle → External metrics → Skepticism → Overcome → External victory → Internal victory), hook crítico
- Man in the street: quick soundbites, 10 core questions × 10+ responses, fast-hitting compilations
- Event logistics: recording, storage, labeling, incentives

**Cita ancla** (Cap. 3): «Good thing there are people who will work in exchange for money»

### Cap. 4 · Communication Scrape + Bonuses + Awards + Competitions

- Support chats: key terms (PIF, Sales, $, Profit, Revenue, close) → screenshots → folder
- Community wins: "wins" category, Friday posts, screen recording compilations
- Reviews: Glassdoor, Yelp, Google, Facebook, Trustpilot → folder
- Bonus unlocks: giveaway → sell OR unlock gratis con testimonial <5 min
- Award testimonials: CUATRO formas de proof (post numbers, video email, unboxing, on-stage)
- Competitions: raw iPhone testimonial contest, medido por conversiones 30 días

**Cita ancla** (Cap. 4): «People like to get recognized in meaningful ways for their meaningful results.»

### Cap. 5 · Putting It All Together + Final Thoughts

- Weekly checklist: 8 tipos de proof-based content (names, social, chats, community, reviews, bonuses, awards, reactions)
- Every 6 months: big influx de events + competitions
- Implementation order: Competition → Chat/Social/Reviews/Community → Bonuses/Awards → Events → Lifecycle
- Insight: "We've never made less by giving more"
- Leverage = hacer más dinero de actividad ya existente

**Cita ancla** (Cap. 5): «prospective customers want to see the results of real customers anyways»
