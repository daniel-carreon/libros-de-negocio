# Alex Hormozi — *$100M Playbook: Fast Cash* (2025)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto. Citación por capítulo (epub sin paginación).

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. Fast Cash Plays son promotions limitadas (scarcity, urgency, exclusivity) dirigidas a warm audiences (clientes actuales, previos, leads engaged) para extraer máximo dinero en mínimo tiempo; el mecanismo es vender 10x el precio a 10% del customer base = duplicar revenue con cero acquisition cost.

2. La estructura es: 1–3 core components + 3 bonuses (layered), precio 10x–50x, spots capped a 5–10% de clientes, comunicación 5–7 días vía email+SMS, con dos opciones: consultations OR automated checkout, ambas first-come-first-serve.

3. Ejecutado quarterly (cada 90 días) con ~15% de annual revenue genera ~50% del profit; ROI ejemplo: $25k MRR gym → +$229k cash/year de fast cash plays = quadruples net income de $75k a $281k sin escalar ads.

## Por capítulo

### Cap. 0 · El Descubrimiento (Líneas 76–160)

- Abril 2019: clientes de Hormozi (gym owners) reciben tax bills inesperados
- Paradoja: ganaron dinero con Gym Launch pero lo gastaron; no tienen cash para impuestos + Hormozi
- Solución: "Fast Cash Offer" - venta de alto ticket a clientes existentes (membresía $5k/año)
- Equipo resistente ("no saben cerrar expensive"): Hormozi agrega sales training
- Resultado: promedio $30k/gym, algunos $50k+; todos renuevan
- Insight filosofía: "Revenue is vanity. Profit is sanity. Cash is reality."

**Cita ancla** (cap. 0): «Revenue is vanity. Profit is sanity. Cash is reality»

### Cap. 1 · What Are Fast Cash Plays (Líneas 182–256)

- **Fast Cash Plays** = limited-time offers a warm audiences (current customers, past customers, engaged leads)
- Distinto de: nurturing (content emails), main offers
- Diseño: maximum $ en minimum time de gente most-likely-to-buy = sin acquisition cost
- Características: scarcity (spots limitados), urgency (deadline), exclusivity (solo warm), big ticket (10x-50x)
- **10x The 10% Rule**: si 10% de customers pagan 10x el precio → duplicas revenue
- Ejemplo: 10 customers × $100 = $1k SAME como 1 customer × $1,000 = $1k (pero con profit)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 1): «all the extra revenue (minus delivery) drops straight to the bottom line.»

### Cap. 2 · Why Fast Cash Matters (Líneas 261–287)

- **Crisis esperables**: equipo roto, tuberías, cyber attacks, abogados, pay periods (cash drain)
- Fast Cash Plays = "get out of jail free" cards (safety net)
- **Profit impact**: en early days, ~15% annual revenue pero ~50% annual profit
- Doblaba take-home income sin escalar

**Cita ancla** (cap. 2): «The only thing better than making extra cash when you need it, is making even more when you don't»

### Cap. 3 · The Fast Cash Playbook (Líneas 292–365)

**Who**: existing customers, previous customers, engaged leads
- No acquisition cost
- Highest conversion (warm)
- Shortest sales cycle

**What to Sell**: unscalable, high-value (pick 1–3 cores + 3 bonuses):
- Attention: 1-on-1 or many-on-1 (high leverage)
- Personalization: custom versions
- Convenience: 24/7, extended hours
- Status: badges, recognition, special parking
- Duration: 6–12 months (vs standard)
- Speed: priority, <10 min response time
- Experience: white glove, dinners, gifts, cell phone access
- Access: retreats, behind-the-scenes, in-person
- Network: connections with other top customers
- Secrets: exclusive bonuses, trade secrets, vendor intros

**How Many Spots**: cap 5–10% de customers (want to SELL OUT fast para credibilidad)

**How to Sell**:
- High price → consultations (personal touch)
- Medium price → consultations OR automated (depends on brand)
- Low price → automated
- **Rule**: limited spots, first-come-first-serve (create stampede)

**What to Charge**: 10x–50x current price
- "Take your average cart value and add a zero"
- "If you want to get frisky, multiply it by five"
- Beyond comparison to other products (price as quality signal)
- Will earn that premium (you work harder for these customers)

**Promote**: 5–7 days or until sold out

**Cita ancla** (cap. 3): «Take whatever your average cart value - or transaction size is, and add a zero.»

### Cap. 4a · PUSH TO CONSULT SEQUENCE (5–7 días)

- Day 7 OUT: Teaser vía texto ("something big coming tomorrow")
- Day 6 OUT: Announcement email + CTA consult (price range if weak sales)
  - SMS to draw attention to email
- Day 5 OUT: Recap bullets + Bonus 1
- Day 3 OUT: Sales update (how many sold, spots left, first-come-first-serve)
- Day 2 OUT: "X Spots Left" + Bonus 2 (escalates urgency)
- Day 1 AM: "Last few hours" + Best Bonus (99% of closes happen here)
- Day 1 PM: "Sold Out" notification
- (Optional) Backout: If someone cancels, text alert (creates second wave)

**Key**: spread consults across window (don't do all same day), all consultations END before deadline

**Cita candidata** (cap. 4a): «The point is you want to sell out fast. This makes your fast cash offer even more compelling the next time»

### Cap. 4b · PUSH TO AUTOMATED CHECKOUT SEQUENCE (5 días)

- Day 5 OUT: Primer text ("something big coming tomorrow")
- Day 4 OUT: Announcement email (1–3 cores, price range)
  - SMS to draw attention
- Day 2 OUT: "Cart opens in 48 hours" + reminder first-come-first-serve
- Day 1 OUT: Bonus 1 + "set an alarm" (expect to sell out in minutes)
- 60 MIN OUT: "set alarm, have payment ready"
- 5 MIN MARK: CART OPENS + Best Bonus (most immediate benefit)
  - SMS alert
- 60 MIN POST: Sales update (how many sold, spots left)
- 6 H POST: "X Spots Left" + New bonus + benefits (good stuff you get, bad stuff avoided)
- 12 H POST: "Sold Out" notification
- (Optional) Backout: If someone cancels

**Key**: build hype → open cart to everyone at once (stampede) → close fast

**Cita candidata** (cap. 4b): «Build up tension, cause a stampede by opening the cart to everyone at once»

### Cap. 5 · Ultra-Premium Examples (Líneas 448–494)

**B2C Fitness - $10k/year**:
- Core: 1-on-1 Year-Long "Total Transformation" (meals, workouts, photoshoot)
- Bonus 1: Nutrition plan + meal prep company intro
- Bonus 2: 1-on-1 workouts 3x/week
- Bonus 3: 24/7 personal cell phone access

**B2B Marketing Agency - $50k/year**:
- Core: 1-Year Personal Brand Explosion (script, film, edit, post 4x/year)
- Bonus 1: Additional social platform
- Bonus 2: Lead magnet creation
- Bonus 3: Organic funnel + video sales letters

**Recurring/Subscription - Price: Monthly × 12**:
- Core: 1 Year to [Ultimate Result]
- Bonus 1: Custom work on struggle area
- Bonus 2: Speed-up thing
- Bonus 3: Guarantee-like thing (virtually guaranteed, not legal guarantee)

**Key Insight**: no need to sell many; unscalable high-touch justifies crazy prices; you're working anyway, get paid more

**Cita ancla** (cap. 5): «Any fool can do less and charge less. But, we're trying to do more so we can charge more»

### Cap. 6 · 5 Reasons to Run Every 90 Days (Líneas 533–585)

**Reason 1**: Cleans pipeline de qualified-but-on-fence leads (big offer + urgency/scarcity = decision)

**Reason 2**: Increases customer value (repeat purchasers stay longer, lifetime value ↑)

**Reason 3**: "Something new and interesting" maintains engagement (psychological)

**Reason 4**: Time to deliver & reset before next play (cadence, sustainable)

**Reason 5**: All extra revenue = bottom line profit (already break-even; warm leads = $0 acq cost; high margin)

**ROI Benchmark** ($25k MRR gym, 166 members @ $150/mo, 25% margins):
- OLD: $300k annual revenue, $75k profit
- Fast Cash 4x/year: ~$229k additional cash ($60k + $65k + $48k + $56k)
- NEW: $529k revenue, $281k profit (QUADRUPLED)
- Bonus: 22 new customers (referrals from fast cash buyers)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 6): «A 76% increase in revenue that yields a quadrupling of net income and 22 new customers»

### Cap. 7 · Checklist & Sample Sequence (Líneas 621–904)

**Fast Cash Checklist**:
- [ ] Pick list (current + past customers)
- [ ] Pick offer (max unscalable value)
- [ ] Pick price (10x–50x, be bold)
- [ ] Pick sales method (consult vs automated)
- [ ] Prep emails + texts (8–10 messages)
- [ ] Send sequence
- [ ] Collect cash
- [ ] Deliver
- [ ] Collect 4Rs: Reviews, Results, Referrals, Resells
- [ ] Cooldown list (back to value content)
- [ ] Set next date (quarter cadence)

**Sample Email Sequence** (Consult-based, 7 days):
- **Day 6**: "We need to talk" (problem: tax bill, solution: prepay + savings)
- **Day 5**: "best. deal. ever." (recap offer, spots going)
- **Day 3**: "how to [goal]" (half gone, new bonus)
- **Day 2**: "2 spots left + game changer" (8 committed, last bonus preview)
- **Day 1 AM**: "Saving $XXX ends tonight" (philosophy: priorities, value, why now)
- **Day 1 PM**: Backout email (someone canceled, one more spot open)

**Copy patterns**:
- Open with pain (tax bill, goal gap)
- Solution + transparency ("I'm investing in X, benefits you because Y")
- Savings + exclusives (20% off year, premium features)
- Urgency (10 spots, expires Saturday midnight, first-come-first-serve)
- CTA (book call, reply "I'm in", text number)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 7): «I'm in. Yes, charge the card you have on file for a year with the discount applied»

## Para Daniel

El playbook operacionaliza el **diagnóstico RACE** para oferta adicional (upsell): "¿Qué cosa high-ticket + unscalable satisfaría la Pain que encontramos?" Las **dos secuencias** (consult vs automated) mapean a consultores que venden 1-on-1 (consultor mismo) vs consultores escalados (equipo). El **4R framework** (Reviews, Results, Referrals, Resells) es medidor de diagnóstico post-venta (feedback loop). Para **Arbrain**: "Fast Cash Sequence Builder" (fill-in blanks: pricing, bonuses, days) acelera consultores nuevos. Para el **Acompañamiento**: Módulo días 45–60 (Monetización de Warm List) enseña que el dinero más fácil es de clientes que ya confían, no de publicidad nueva.
