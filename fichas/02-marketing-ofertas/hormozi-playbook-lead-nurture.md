# Alex Hormozi — *$100M Playbook: Lead Nurture* (2025)

> Ficha de lectura: mapa para ir al libro, no sustituto. Páginas = número de página **del PDF** (el que muestra el visor, no el impreso), salvo que `citar_por` diga capítulo.

## Tesis en tres líneas

1. **Show rate (% de scheduled leads que llegan) multiplica revenue directo**: 20-40% mejora en show rate = 2-3x profit anual; metrics: schedule rate (engaged → appointment), show rate (appointment → arrival), throughput (engaged → arrival); mayoría propietarios ignoran porque piensan no pueden hacer nada, no saben qué hacer, o creen hay work más importante.

2. **Cuatro pilares maximizan show rate: Availability (slots abiertos) > Speed (contact <5min) > Personalization (mensaje relevante cada lead) > Volume (reach-out 7+ times)**; data ALAN (Vlad machine learning) descubrió: availability es biggest lever (almost perfect correlation con throughput), speed to first contact = 391% conversion lift si <60 seg, personalization = reciprocidad + remove risk, volume = abandon after 1.3 attempts es default.

3. **Ejecución = cultura, no procesos: Jacob "garbage man" reframe unqualified leads como "free practice" + "yellows are new gold", volume + boring work + BAMFAM (book-meeting-from-meeting) genera highest show rates; métricas (show rate + close rate + lead-to-close %), incentivos (5-30% bonus), isms ("volume negates luck") crean comportamientos que escalan**.

## Por capítulo

### Cap. 1 · Intro - COVID crisis (líneas 116–203)

- 2020: COVID cierra gyms, Hormozi tenía ALAN (appointment-setting software) sólo para gyms; pivotó a marketing agencies
- Contrató Vlad (data scientist, machine learning Olympics); Vlad encontró 4 data points correlacionados con show-ups:
  - Más availability → más shows
  - Menos días para booking → más shows
  - Más follow-ups → más shows
  - Leads responden más a follow-ups → más shows
- Insight: show rate es critical porque → más shows = más ventas; negocio owners ignoran; 20-40% mejora = 2-3x anual profit

**Cita ancla** (cap. 1): «If they do not show, they cannot buy.»

### Cap. 2 · Definitions (líneas 210–239)

- Tres métricas: schedule rate (engaged leads que appointment), show rate (scheduled que show), throughput (total engaged que show)
- Ejemplo: 100 leads → 50% schedule → 50 appointments → 50% show → 25 shows → 25% throughput
- "Money not made" = money lost; reframe crítica para motivación

**Cita ancla** (cap. 2): «Show rates matter so much because the more people who show up to buy your thing, the more of your thing you'll sell.»

### Cap. 3 · Four Pillars (líneas 251–276)

- Framework basado data ALAN + portfolio (20,000 leads/día):
  1) **Availability**: appointment slots, hours, flexibility
  2) **Speed to contact**: respuesta rápida + far out para schedule
  3) **Personalization**: comunicación útil + relevant
  4) **Volume**: número de veces reach out
- Si haces todos = más schedules + más shows + más compras
- "Golden BBs, no silver bullets"

**Cita ancla** (cap. 3): «You must first become consistent before you can become exceptional.»

### Cap. 4 · Pillar I: Availability (líneas 286–466)

- Anécdota: Leila nail salon LA; salon 1 no answer, 2 no slots, 3 complicated, 4 walk-ins welcome + text confirmation → she went there
- Data: Vlad confirma availability > speed; biggest lever show rate
- Tácticas:
  1) 7 days/week (pay rent 7/7, make money 7/7)
  2) More hours/day (6am-6pm PST covers max US sales)
  3) Flexible times (4 options/hour: 12:00, 12:15, 12:30, 12:45)
  4) Inbound + outbound + self-scheduling (all three)
- Self-scheduler UX: header = attention avatar, headline, subheadline; times visible; eliminate steps; personalize (picture > faceless)
- Friction control: too many bad appointments? add video, sales letter, price, delay

**Cita ancla** (cap. 4): «If leads cannot schedule, they do not show.»

### Cap. 5 · Pillar II: Speed (líneas 472–645)

- Stats: 391% lift si contact <60 sec (Velocify), 7x más qualified si <1h vs. 1h later (HBR), 60x vs. 24h, 78% buy from first respondent (Lead Connect)
- Tres componentes:
  1) **Speed to first contact** (<5 min goal, <60 sec extra credit): contact immediately after opt-in; benefit = fewer follow-ups needed
  2) **Speed to first appointment** (max 3 days out): same-day > next-day; script para pull forward: "confirmed Monday BUT opening today 4pm"; hot handoff si different closer
  3) **Speed of response** (lead decide before sales call): responde quickly a messages post-schedule; timestamps everywhere
- Insight: "Money loves speed. Wealth loves time. Poverty loves indecision."

**Cita ancla** (cap. 5): «Money loves speed. Wealth loves time. Poverty loves indecision.»

### Cap. 6 · Pillar III: Personalization (líneas 650–885)

- Anécdota: ALAN beta tester con exceptional show rates; owner texts separately: "I've got shirt here for you, black or pink?"; si responden color → almost always show up
- Seis tácticas:
  1) **Lead's preferred communication method**: start multi-channel (call, text, DM, email), continue donde responden primero; reach out "like trying to reach parents"
  2) **Qualify the leads**: weight loss company cancela bad fit appointments; usa application + scoring; focus time good leads
  3) **Best leads to best closers**: timeshare #1 rep: "give best leads" → 5x office year 1 → nationwide → $200M → $1B; score 1-5 o red-yellow-green; route best to best
  4) **Segment messaging**: HubSpot 7x email ROI after segmenting; easy = 5 min research per lead, hard = different sequences per category; pro tips: iPhone blue messages (not green spam), local area code call wrapping
  5) **Incentivize showing**: push incentive (gift card before, reciprocity, whether show or not), pull incentive (A/B choice when show: quantity, start date, type, payment, flavor, time, media, delivery, size, color, material, personnel, communication)
  6) **Proof**: testimonials + case studies match demographics; weave: after schedule, between calls, if ghost (90-day reminder)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 6): «Figure out what leads want, get that thing for them, and then tell them they can have it if they show up.»

### Cap. 7 · Pillar IV: Volume (líneas 890–1000)

- Insight: Nearly half salespeople abandon after first attempt; leads opted in = dieron permission para contact
- Definición: number of times reach out before giving up
- Tres componentes:
  1) **Scheduling appointments** (7+ touchpoints week 1): call <5 min, double-dial (1st blocked?), voicemail, text post-VM, double dial + text 2x más day 1, call 2x days 2-3 (early + late), text post-2nd call, call + text 1x/day days 4-7, after week 1 → long-term nurture
  2) **Reminding to show** (automated + manual): automated (immediate confirm, 24h, 12h, 3h out; include area code = 2x pickup), manual (night before, morning of, 60 min prior, blue messages, short messages vs. blocky)
  3) **Book next at current** (BAMFAM): never "circle back later"; both there now → pull calendar, pick time; if can't schedule with them then = certainly won't after

**Cita ancla** (cap. 7): «Nearly half of all salespeople give up after the first attempt. No bueno. Time to change that.»

### Cap. 8 · Execution (líneas 1015–1170)

- Anécdota: Jacob 15-year-old, wants make money; cold call job 150-call quota; told do 2x top guy (400/day), work 6x if he works 5, no breaks; objection "leads unqualified" → reframe "free practice, yellows are new gold, volume negates luck"
- Jacob rose to #1 salesman (26 guys) before Director Sales Acquisition.com
- Culture frame: rules governing good/bad behavior
- Behaviors good: available, fast, personal, persistent; bad: unavailable, slow, boring, fragile
- Incentivos: monetary (5-30% bonus show rate), non-monetary (attention, affection, approval, isms: "boring work," "volume negates luck," "speed is king")
- Métricas por rep: show rate (work ethic), close rate (skill), lead-to-close % (both); track all three
- Insight: best closers typically best setters (same skillset, higher stakes)

**Cita ancla** (cap. 8): «Jacob rose through the ranks and finished his tenure at Gym Launch as the top salesman the quarter he left, out of 26 guys.»

### Cap. 9 · Recap (líneas 1153–1170)

- Five-point summary: 1) Convenience, 2) Speed, 3) Personalization, 4) Volume, 5) Execute
- Checklist completo ejecutable

**Cita ancla** (cap. 9): «With that said, I hope you use this to grow your business and prosper.»
